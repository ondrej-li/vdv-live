The app

The map, a service in detail, a stop's departure board. All in Czech and English, all working offline.

What it does

A live map of the region

Every vehicle the region reports, coloured by how late it is. The camera is locked to Vysočina, so you cannot lose your way.

Departure boards

Pick a stop and see what leaves it: the timetable time, the expected one, and the delay. Narrow it to the lines you pinned.

The official timetables

Every stop of a run, with the times from the Ministry of Transport's archive moved by the live delay. Fetched per line, kept on the phone.

Departure reminders

Choose a service, a stop and how early you want to know. The reminder follows the days the service runs, public holidays, and the end of the timetable.

Apple Watch

The vehicles of your pinned lines on your wrist, coloured by delay. The watch fetches its own data and does not need the phone.

Nothing is collected

No account, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs. Your position stays on the device.

Widgets

A departure board for the stop you choose, and the pinned lines' vehicles nearest to you, on the home screen.

On the wrist

Only what you care about: your pinned lines and their vehicles.
