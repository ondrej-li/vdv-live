# VDV Live

Live buses and trains in the Vysočina Region, and their published timetables.

Živé polohy autobusů a vlaků v Kraji Vysočina a jejich zveřejněné jízdní řády.

## Documents · Dokumenty

- [Privacy policy · Zásady ochrany osobních údajů](privacy.html)
- [Support · Podpora](support.html)

VDV Live is an unofficial app: it is not operated by the region, by the Ministry
of Transport, or by any transport operator. Positions come from the regional map
of the Vysočina Region at mapavdv.kr-vysocina.cz; timetables come from
*Jízdní řády veřejné linkové dopravy*, published by the Ministry of Transport of
the Czech Republic on portal.cisjr.cz under CC BY 4.0; stop positions are matched
from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0); the base map is
Apple Maps.
